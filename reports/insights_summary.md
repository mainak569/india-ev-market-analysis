## 1. Situation

- Client: a two-wheeler EV brand planning to expand. Question: how fast is E2W adoption growing,
  where, who holds share, and which states to prioritise.
- Data: Vahan registrations (all 36 states/UTs), Jan 2023 - Aug 2026; population projections (MoHFW, 1 Oct 2025).
- E2W registrations by FY:
  - FY2023-24: 1,000,612 (penetration 5.49%)
  - FY2024-25: 1,210,921 (+21.0%, penetration 6.17%)
  - FY2025-26: 1,468,093 (+21.2%, penetration 6.62%)
  - FY2026-27 Apr-Aug: 916,256 vs 530,286 same period last year (+72.8%), penetration 9.90%

- Monthly penetration crossed 10% for the first time in Jun 2026 (10.59%); Jul 2026 11.24%

## 5. Data limitations

- Registrations, not sales; low-speed E2W (<= 25 km/h, <= 250 W) are not registered and not counted
- September 2026 excluded (incomplete at download on 28 Sep 2026)
- Population is a single projection (1 Oct 2025), applied to all years
- Maker names are legal entities; renames merged by hand (e.g. Ampere Vehicles -> Greaves Electric Mobility,
  Chetak Technology -> Bajaj Auto)
- State-level maker data covers 10 states (82% of 2023 E2W volume); the rest is a "Rest of India" bucket
